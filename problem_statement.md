The current system provides route optimization results primarily through visual representation on a map, where stations are connected to their respective visits. While this enables intuitive understanding of routes, it lacks a structured and exportable format for further analysis, reporting and operational use.

1. Lack of Exportable Output
At present, there is no mechanism to export the generated routing solution into a standardized file format. This limits the ability of users to:
Perform offline analysis
Share results with stakeholders
Integrate outputs with external systems (e.g., reporting tools, logistics dashboards)

2. Absence of Detailed Route-Level Data Representation
The visual map does not provide a comprehensive tabular breakdown of the optimized solution. Specifically, the system does not expose structured details such as:
The origin station for each delivery
The destination visit
The priority/order of execution for visits within a route
The previous and next stops in the sequence
The arrival time at each visit
The departure time from each visit
This lack of granular visibility makes it difficult to audit routes, validate optimization results and support operational planning.

Objective
To enhance the system by introducing an export functionality that generates the optimized routing output as an Excel file (.xlsx) containing a structured, row-level representation of each stop in the route.
The exported data must include:
Origin (station)
Destination (visit)
Visit priority/order
Previous stop
Next stop
Arrival time
Departure time
This improvement aims to provide greater transparency, usability and interoperability of the optimization results beyond the map-based visualization.
